Overview of eggNOG

The eggNOG (evolutionary genealogy of genes: Non-supervised Orthologous Groups) computational tool, often used via eggNOG-mapper, is a, high-throughput functional annotation tool that assigns functional descriptions to novel protein sequences (from genomes, transcriptomes, or metagenomes) based on orthology rather than simple sequence similarity. Its basic principle relies on mapping query sequences to a precomputed database of Orthologous Groups (OGs) spanning all three domains of life, allowing for more precise functional transfer compared to traditional BLAST-based approaches.
